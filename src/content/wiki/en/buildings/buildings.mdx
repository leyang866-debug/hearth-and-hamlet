## What buildings do

Buildings turn raw resources and workers into a more capable settlement. Early sites establish food and timber production; later structures expand housing, unlock specialist work, and open research, policy, trade, magic, or defensive options.

## A practical building order

1. Build the production and camp upgrades required by the opening objectives.
2. Add housing or capacity when population and storage block progress.
3. Unlock the specialist building that removes the next actual bottleneck.
4. Keep enough resources for required quests and defense rather than spending all stockpiles on side upgrades.

## Building categories

- **Camp and housing:** support population and core settlement growth.
- **Production:** gather food, timber, stone, and other needed resources.
- **Specialists:** extend crafting, research, magic, trade, and other systems.
- **Defense:** prepare the settlement for later threats.

## FAQ

### What should I build first?

Follow the current opening objective, then prioritize food stability and the resource production needed for the next meaningful unlock.

### Should I upgrade every building immediately?

No. Upgrade when it solves a current capacity, production, or unlock requirement.

### Do buildings affect defense?

Later game systems include defensive preparation. Preserve resources for mandatory defense-related goals when they appear.
