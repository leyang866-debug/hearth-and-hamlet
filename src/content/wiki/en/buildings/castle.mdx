## Castle planning

The supplied materials establish that the game grows from a frontier camp into a kingdom with larger structures, including a castle. They do not verify a fixed castle cost, worker requirement, or permanent bonus.

## Decision checklist

1. Confirm the current unlock condition in the building panel.
2. Check whether food, storage, or defense is blocking current progress.
3. Save the resources required for the castle only when it is the next meaningful unlock.
4. Rebalance workers after construction if the new building changes your resource needs.

## What may change

Exact castle effects are version-dependent unless confirmed by current official material. Avoid using old screenshots or another player's values as a guaranteed build plan.

## FAQ

### Is there a castle in Hearth and Hamlet?

Yes. The supplied research identifies a castle as part of the game's building progression.

### What does the castle unlock?

The available sources do not confirm a current, complete effect list. Check the in-game building details.

### When should I save for the castle?

Save when its unlock condition is reachable and it is more valuable than the production, food, or defense need currently blocking your settlement.
