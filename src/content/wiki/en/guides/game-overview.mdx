## Quick answer

Hearth and Hamlet is developed by Phorust Studios and published by Runic Forge and Gamersky Games. Steam lists it as a casual simulation and strategy game with support for Windows, SteamOS, and Linux.

## Core gameplay loop

1. Gather the immediate resource needed for construction.
2. Assign workers to keep food and other production stable.
3. Build, upgrade, and research to unlock the next layer of the settlement.
4. Prepare for the later systems: policies, trade, magic, alchemy, and defense.

## Platforms and languages

Steam lists single-player support, Windows, SteamOS, and Linux. Available store languages include English, Simplified Chinese, German, Japanese, Russian, Korean, and Traditional Chinese.

## PC requirements

The listed minimum requirement is Windows 10 64-bit, an Intel Core i3 at 3.2 GHz or AMD equivalent, 8 GB RAM, a GTX 650 2 GB or Radeon HD 7770 2 GB, DirectX 11, and 1 GB of storage. Check the Steam listing before installing because requirements can change.

## What may change

Building costs, balance, and optimal research choices can change with patches. This wiki avoids presenting those values as permanent without a version-specific source.

## FAQ

### Is Hearth and Hamlet an incremental game?

Yes. Worker production and upgrades form an incremental loop, while manual gathering can help with short-term resource gaps.

### Is Hearth and Hamlet single-player?

Yes. Steam lists the game as single-player.

### Who develops Hearth and Hamlet?

Phorust Studios is the listed developer. Runic Forge and Gamersky Games are listed as publishers.
