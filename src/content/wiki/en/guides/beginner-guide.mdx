## Opening priorities

Start with the opening requirements, then turn new population into worker assignments as soon as it is practical. Food is the first persistent constraint because population growth raises consumption.

## A reliable early route

1. Gather the resources required by the current objective.
2. Stabilize food production before accelerating population growth.
3. Move a small number of workers to the resource blocking the next building or research.
4. Spend or expand storage before a production line reaches its cap.
5. Recheck worker assignments after every major unlock.

## Resource decisions

Do not equalize every resource by default. A large timber stockpile is less useful than the food or stone required for the next meaningful unlock. Watch the production trend as well as the total stockpile.

## Research and defense

Choose permanent research that removes the current bottleneck. Leave enough production capacity for mandatory objectives and later defense instead of spending every resource on optional upgrades.

## FAQ

### What should I prioritize first in Hearth and Hamlet?

Prioritize food stability, then the specific resource required by the next useful building or research purchase.

### Should I assign every worker to one resource?

No. Adjust workers in small steps and watch whether the resource trend becomes safe for the next objective.

### When should I increase storage?

Increase storage when a key production line is capped or when the next planned purchase requires more capacity.
